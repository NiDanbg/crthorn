The third and, sadly, final installment of the Chronicles of the Chrysanthemum trilogy is finally out! You can find it on Amazon and across all D2D retail partners.
It was an absolute pleasure working on Empress—Rami’s character still lingers in my mind. In fact, while editing the book, I couldn’t resist writing three prequel short stories following Rami, Akira, and Kenjiro before the events of the main story.
You can download them completely free of charge—you’ll find the link on the page of each book in the series.
Happy reading!

The third and, sadly, final part of the "Chrysanthemum Chronicles" trilogy is finally here! You can purchase it directly from here or find it on Amazon and through D2D's partners.
Working on "Empress" was an absolute pleasure for me — Rami's character still lingers in my mind. While editing the book, I couldn't help myself and decided to write three short prequels dedicated to Rami, Akira, and Kenjiro, set before the events of the main story.
You can download them completely for free — you will find a link on the page of each book in the series.
Happy reading!

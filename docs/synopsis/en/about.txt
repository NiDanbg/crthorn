Krasimir Tenev
(also writing under the pen name Crispin Thorn)
Krasimir Tenev is a writer born and raised in Burgas, Bulgaria. An avid reader of authors like Stanisław Lem and Isaac Asimov, and classics such as "The Lord of the Rings" and "The Belgariad." He has watched "Star Wars," "Indiana Jones," and all the classics of the genre.
He graduated from the Faculty of Law at Burgas Free University. After graduating, he moved to Sofia, where he began to build the world of Boria.

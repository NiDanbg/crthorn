For centuries, the fallen angel Beelzebub has found refuge in anonymity. Under the guise of Billy, a skilled chef, he suppresses his divine nature and strives to atone for the sins of a turbulent past through the simple joys of cooking. But the past never stays buried forever.
Small, uncontrollable cracks in his mortal mask turn him into a threat—not only to himself but also to the few he cares for.
In The Key of Abundance, the second book of the breathtaking The Age of the Fallen series, the stakes are higher than ever. Hunted by fanatics and entangled in courtly intrigues, Billy must flee. But escape is no longer enough. Whispers of an ancient relic—The Abundant Horn, his Horn—drift through the shadows. An artifact of unimaginable power, capable of turning deserts into gardens or bringing famine and ruin. A power everyone craves.
Meanwhile, Lucifer, his brother, gathers strength, and his machinations threaten to reignite the war that caused their Fall. Their paths are bound to cross.
Will Billy manage to preserve his humanity, or will he have to embrace his demonic past to survive? Can he trust anyone in a world drowning in betrayal? And what is the price one must pay to reclaim what was lost?
Dive into the second installment of The Age of the Fallen saga—an epic tale of power, redemption, and the demons we carry within.

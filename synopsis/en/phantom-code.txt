In the rain-drenched streets of Neo-Serdica, death is no longer the end. It’s a business.
The megacorporation Eternity Solutions has turned eternal life into a commodity, preserving the consciousness of the deceased in digital archives. The wealthy can converse with their Digital Ghosts forever. But in the neon-soaked smog where technology and corruption entwine, some secrets are better left buried.
Kalin Valchev—Kalata—is a private detective of the old school. Cynical, haunted by his own ghosts, and drowning his past in cheap whiskey, he’s a relic in a world where truth is rewritten with code. His office reeks of failure, and clients are rare.  
Everything changes when an anonymous patron deposits a hefty sum into his account with one simple task: investigate the “suicide” of Emil Kirilov, a brilliant programmer from Eternity Solutions.  
The case plunges Kalata into a ruthless world of corporate espionage, black markets for digital souls, and the sinister Project Mnemosyne—a technology with the potential to alter the very essence of human memory and identity.  
In a city where memories can be erased and ghosts can be manipulated, the truth is the most dangerous contraband.  
Phantom Code is a gripping cyberpunk noir thriller that will keep you on edge until the last page. Perfect for fans of classic crime tales transplanted into a dark, yet eerily plausible future.

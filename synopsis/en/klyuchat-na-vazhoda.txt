Who pays when a proud man decides for everyone?

Lucius wakes in a forest outside the city of Altara with no name, no past and a pack of dogs closing in. The boy's body is nineteen. His hands know how to fight. A wanderer woman reads his palm, says one word, "fallen," and sends him to find a seer who is chained in the stables of the street king Rex Magnus, called the Butcher.

Seven angels fell. Each was reborn in a human body with its memory wiped, and each carries a sin that is the twisted shape of a gift. Lucius's sin is pride. Every power that wakes in him answers to a command, and every command he gives in the lower streets of Altara costs someone else. The seer Cassandra wants only one thing: to reach the Cursed Well and drink until the visions stop. Lan, a starved young gang leader, wants to keep his children out of Rex's hands. And Captain Arnold von Baden of the Order of the Prophet Elijah has ridden into the city to finish what his Order started twelve years ago.

At the end of the road lies a Key that can be taken only by turning the sin around. The Order wants it first. So does everyone Lucius has already failed.

How many of them will he have to bury before he learns what the Key asks of him?

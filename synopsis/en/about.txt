Crispin Thorn

Crispin Thorn was born and raised in Burgas, Bulgaria. An avid reader of Stanisław Lem and Isaac Asimov, and of classics like "The Lord of the Rings" and "The Belgariad," he grew up on "Star Wars," "Indiana Jones," and every other genre staple worth watching.
He studied law at Burgas Free University. After graduating, he moved to Sofia, where he began building the world of Boria.

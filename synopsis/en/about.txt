Crispin Thorn

Crispin Thorn (pen name)

Born and raised in the city of Burgas, Bulgaria, he is one of those people who discovered the endless worlds between the covers of a book at an early age. His favorite authors are the science fiction masters Stanisław Lem and Isaac Asimov, whose ideas taught him to ask the big questions about the future, the mind, and the limits of human knowledge. He is just as captivated by the epic fantasy worlds of The Lord of the Rings and The Belgariad, where adventure, friendship, and the struggle between good and evil come alive in full force.

He also loves the cinematic adventures of his childhood. Star Wars and Indiana Jones remain, for him, proof of how a story can awaken the imagination and make you dream of distant galaxies and forgotten treasures.

He graduated in law, yet never stopped searching for his own stories beyond statutes and paragraphs. Today, he continues to turn his love of grand adventure into worlds of his own.

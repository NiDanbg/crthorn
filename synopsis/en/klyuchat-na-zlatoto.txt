What would you pay never to lose anything again?
Matthias has never lost a bargain. He holds the old debts of Gula's crown, plays chess against nobody, and knows the price of every cask in Merante's cellars—down to the man who grew the grapes. Thirty years ago, he woke on the gravel under the city, a barefoot boy with no name and no past. Since then, he has bought everything else.
Seven angels fell. Each was reborn with every memory wiped, carrying a sin that is the twisted shape of a gift. Matthias's sin is greed—he cannot bear to lose. He hires Isabella, a mercenary thief, to steal a page from the archive on Silver Hill. That same night, a seven-voice choir begins to sing beneath his windows, and the Order of the Prophet Elijah—hunters of the fallen—already knows his name. Across the sea lies Avaritia, the kingdom where every well has a padlock and every freedom is bought for a year. In its free port of the Azure Chamber, the Order is already buying out the city ahead of him. Meanwhile, a brother he has never met is closing the distance between them, and every mile makes both of them bleed.
The Key waits behind a scale that weighs only what hurts to lose. And Matthias has spent his life making sure someone else pays.
Who will stand on the scale when the account comes due?
The Key of Gold is Book 3 of The Age of the Fallen—a gritty, character-driven dark epic fantasy of merchant intrigue, cursed bargains, and magic with a moral price.

THE EMPIRE IS BURNING. AND THE CROWN IS HEAVIER THAN ANY ARMOR.
Princess Rami has stepped out of the shadows, taking command of the loyalist fleet as the Regent of the Hinode Empire. But the brutal Shogun Koriyama’s armada is relentless, and his spies are buried deep within her own ranks. Outnumbered, outgunned, and bleeding, Rami's forces are pushed to the absolute brink of annihilation.

To turn the tide, Rami must embark on a desperate mission to a forgotten, dead world. What she finds hidden in the darkness could end the war in a single day: an ancient alien artifact of unfathomable destructive power.

But absolute power demands an agonizing price. With elite assassins hunting her and her closest allies paying in blood, Rami faces the ultimate test of leadership. If she unleashes the artifact, she could wipe out the enemy armada and save billions—but at the cost of her own humanity.

Will she sacrifice her soul to win the war, or risk losing everything to do what is right?

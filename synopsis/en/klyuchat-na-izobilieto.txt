Who goes hungry when a cook cannot stop giving?

Billy Moss is the finest cook at the court of Gula. His sauces make lords weep, and the gold sparks drifting through them are no trick of the kitchen. Fifteen years ago, he woke in a grown man's body with no past—only scattered fragments he cannot place: a small hand pushing away a fruit, a threshold, an empty palm. When a prince collapses at the king's feast, Billy has one hour before the Order of the Prophet Elijah comes for the cook. He flees with Feran, a captain barely twenty-four—the only other witness to who poured the cup.
Seven angels fell. Each was reborn with every memory wiped, carrying a sin that is the twisted shape of a gift. Billy's sin is gluttony—the relentless need to be needed. In the mountains, the Cult of the Spear guards the Horn of Abundance, a relic that feeds the starving hills only to keep them on their knees. Prior Silvian offers a bargain: Billy's hands for Feran's life. Captain Arnold von Baden hunts them down, guided by a voice in blue glass demanding the cook alive. And somewhere to the east, a brother Billy cannot meet bleeds harder with every mile he draws near.
The Horn will burn any hand that grabs it. Every plate Billy serves is paid for by someone with a name.
When the hungry kneel and ask, what does it cost to give them only what they can hold?

The Key of Abundance is Book 2 of The Age of the Fallen—a gritty, character-driven dark epic fantasy of cursed relics, royal intrigue, and magic with a moral price.
